# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-21

First public release: AU and VST3 for macOS 11+ (universal: Apple silicon and Intel), VST3 for 64-bit Windows.

- A long stereo record buffer, played back broken on every clock tick: stutters, reverse,
  varispeed, tape stops, skips and dropouts. The clock can be free time, host tempo or MIDI notes.
- Macro mode rolls dice from zones on the Bend and Break knobs. Micro mode makes Bend a direct
  speed and Break a direct slice.
- Corrupt: Decimate, Dropout, Destroy, DJ Filter or Vinyl on top.
- Break this bar: press it, or map a key or a MIDI note, and the bar breaks up and lets go on the
  downbeat. It can also run by itself every 2, 4, 8 or 16 bars, with a chance.
- Modulation: four LFOs, an envelope follower and four MIDI CC lanes, routed by dragging onto a knob.
  Gate notes play Freeze, Bend, Break, Corrupt, Reset and Break bar.
- 60 level-matched factory presets in ten categories, and your own.
- Controls moved by Live's Key Map, MIDI Map or automation light up in the window. A control in a
  closed drawer opens that drawer.
- A safety clipper on the output (on by default) and an output trim.
